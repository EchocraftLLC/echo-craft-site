Common questions

Where are you based, and how far do you travel?

I'm based near Easton, Allentown, and Bethlehem, PA, but I travel across the state for sessions and events.

How long until I get my photos?

Portrait and family sessions are delivered within 2–3 weeks. Event coverage is delivered within 1–2 weeks, and a small preview set is usually ready within a few days. Fashion and editorial timelines depend on the project, and we’ll agree on a delivery date when you book. Every gallery arrives through a private Pixieset link made just for you, with high-resolution, edited images you can download and share.

Do you require a deposit to book?

Yes. A 50% non-refundable retainer is due when you book, and it secures your date. The remaining balance is due on the day of the session (or before the event begins). Until the retainer is paid, your date is not held. I accept payment by card or digital payment, and I’ll send an invoice and a simple agreement after you inquire.

What should I wear or bring to my session?

Solid colors and simple patterns tend to photograph best, and layers give us options to work with. Happy to talk through outfit ideas once your session is booked.

What if I need to reschedule?

Life happens. You can reschedule once at no charge with at least 48 hours’ notice, and your retainer carries over to the new date. Cancellations are non-refundable, and the retainer covers the time I’ve held for you. If I need to move a session for weather or an emergency, we’ll reschedule at no cost. Sessions that run late because of a late arrival may end on time so I can honor the next booking.
