Session prep guide

A few simple choices make a big difference. Here is how to feel ready.

What to wear

  • Pick solid colors and simple patterns. They photograph best and keep the focus on you.
  • Choose colors that work together, not matching outfits. Think a shared palette, such as creams, greens, and denim.
  • Bring layers like a jacket, cardigan, or scarf. They add depth and give us options.
  • Avoid big logos and busy graphics, unless that is the point of your shoot.
  • Wear what you feel confident and comfortable in. It always shows.

Hair, makeup, and details

  • Style your hair the way you normally would, or a little more polished.
  • Keep makeup natural, with a touch more than usual since cameras soften things.
  • Press or steam your outfit and check for lint or loose threads the day before.

Families and kids

  • Schedule around nap and snack times. Happy kids make the best photos.
  • Bring a favorite snack or small toy for breaks.
  • Keep it playful. Some of the best images come from tickles, twirls, and silly moments.

Maternity

  • The best timing is usually 28 to 36 weeks.
  • Fitted or flowy pieces that show your shape work well. Bring a partner and kids if you would like them included.

Business and branding

  • Bring two or three outfit options that match your brand and your clients.
  • Tell me where the photos will be used, such as a website, social media, or print, and I will frame them to fit.

On the day

  • Arrive a few minutes early so we can start on time.
  • Bring water, and shoes you can walk in if we are shooting outdoors.
  • Check the weather. We can adjust the plan, and I am happy to reschedule for storms. See the booking page for details.

After your session

Your edited photos arrive in a private online gallery made just for you. Timelines are on the booking page.
