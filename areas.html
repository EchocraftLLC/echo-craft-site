Service areas

Based near Easton, Allentown, and Bethlehem, and happy to travel across Pennsylvania.

Easton

Portrait, family, and maternity sessions in and around Easton. Downtown streets, the riverfront, and local parks all make easy backdrops, and I can help you pick a spot that suits your style and the season.

Bethlehem

The historic downtown and the SteelStacks area offer a mix of old brick, industrial scenery, and open space. Great for branding sessions, couples, and families. Some locations need permission, and I will help sort that out when we plan your session.

Allentown

From downtown murals to the city’s parks and green spaces, Allentown has plenty of variety. I also cover events and festivals here.

Lehigh Valley and beyond

I regularly photograph across the Lehigh Valley and travel throughout Pennsylvania for events, fashion shows, and sessions. Travel may affect your quote, so tell me where you are when you inquire.
