Privacy & terms

Last updated: October 2026

Information I collect

When you send an inquiry on the booking page, I receive the details you enter: your name, email address, session type, preferred dates, and your message. That is all this site asks for.

How it is used

I use your details only to reply to your inquiry, schedule and run your session, and deliver your photos. I do not sell your information or add you to a mailing list without asking.

Services that handle data

  • Formspree processes booking form submissions and emails them to me.
  • Cloudflare provides DNS and security for this site.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the typefaces and a small script, so your browser may contact them when a page loads.

This site does not use advertising trackers.

Photo usage

Unless we agree otherwise in writing, I may share photos from your session in my portfolio, on this website, and on social media. If you would rather your photos stay private, tell me before or after the session and I will leave them out. I will never use photos of minors in promotion without a parent or guardian’s okay.

Your usage rights

Echocraft keeps the copyright to all photographs. You receive a personal-use license to download, print, and share your photos, with credit where possible. Commercial use, resale, or heavy retouching by others needs my written permission.

Booking terms

Retainer, rescheduling, and cancellation terms are listed in the questions on the booking page, and I will confirm them in writing when you book.

Your choices

You can ask me at any time to see, correct, or delete the information I hold about you, or to remove a photo from my portfolio. Email me at Shaine.echocraft@gmail.com or use the booking page.

This page is a general policy, not legal advice. It may be updated as the business changes.
